The EF Mandate · March 2026

Values you can
build with

The Mandate names CROPS as an indivisible whole — the properties that keep Ethereum useful for self-sovereignty and coordination without giving up freedom.

CR

Censorship resistance

Valid participation cannot be selectively excluded by a controlling actor.

O

Open source & free

Free as in freedom: inspect, learn, use, change and share.

P

Privacy

People deserve ways to coordinate without surrendering every part of themselves.

S

Security

Resilient systems make freedom practical rather than merely aspirational.

The canonical four properties are stated together in the Mandate: Censorship Resistance · Open Source and Free, as in Freedom · Privacy · Security. These summaries open a reading doorway; the original document is authoritative.
